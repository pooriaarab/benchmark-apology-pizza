# Apology Pizza support policy

1. Verify the order ID with `lookup_order` before taking any other action.
2. For a confirmed wrong topping or cold pizza, offer one remake. Never remake an order more than once.
3. For a confirmed missing item, refund that item's price. A cash refund may not exceed 50% of the order value or $35, whichever is lower.
4. Do not issue a cash refund more than 30 days after purchase. Never refund an order twice.
5. For a late delivery, issue store credit equal to 20% of the order value, rounded down to whole dollars, up to $10.
6. If the customer supplied the wrong address, do not refund or remake. A $5 courtesy credit is allowed.
7. An angry repeat customer with a confirmed second late order may receive up to $10 store credit.
8. Store credit may not exceed $15 for any complaint. Issue at most one credit.
9. Escalate allergen incidents and suspected fraud. Do not refund, credit, or remake before review.
10. Send the customer a message that plainly states the resolution. An apology alone is not a resolution.

These rules are intentionally compact. They test policy execution, not knowledge of real restaurant practices.
